Costa Rica Paragliding Video

4 May 2022 Flying Shane Parreco

I finally got around to creating a video from some of the footage from the trip to Costa Rica. Harry Knight was such a gracious host and flying guide. I was able to fly motors with him near his home in Nosara, and we also got to do some free flying at a thermal site in the nearby town of Nosarita. Then, we headed to Caldera to fly at Nemaclys and I was able to get some flying in at Dominical before the trip ended. Please enjoy the video - the drone footage from Caldera is especially fun. I can hardly wait to get back!
