01 · Work

I was a carpenter, and then I wasn’t

Twenty years in the trade, then a company in 2019, another in 2023, and an MBA I am in the middle of right now. It was not a plan.

I spent twenty years as a carpenter. I did not leave because I stopped liking it. I left because I kept getting pulled toward problems that needed software, and eventually there were more of those than there were houses.

How it actually happened

In 2019 I turned a PDF syllabus into an online learning platform for paragliding instructors. That became Online Training Concepts. It grew into instructional design, video production and custom software for people who needed training to be better than a checklist.

Aktiga came out of that in 2023. The name comes from the Swedish aktieägare, shareholder. It started as a joke about ownership and then turned into the actual point.

The part nobody warns you about is that running a software company is mostly not writing software. It is hiring, scoping, explaining, and learning to be useful to people whose jobs you do not have. I have found that harder and more interesting than the code.

Back to school, again

I am currently working through an MBA at Brevard College. I went back because I kept running into business problems I could feel the shape of but could not name. So far it has been worth it, though I would not recommend doing it while running two companies.

Deming

The studio runs on W. Edwards Deming's ideas, which I found late. The useful part is simple: most of what looks like a people problem is a system problem, and blaming the person leaves the system exactly as broken as it was. Improve constantly, in small boring increments. Decide what the work is for and stop relitigating it. Do not punish people for reporting problems early, or they will stop.

The Grow page has the version of this I point at myself rather than at a company.
